How to play

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Aim with the mouse. Left click attacks, right click or 1–4 use abilities, R uses your specialization ability. Q drinks a potion, E talks or trades, I opens your character, T your talents, M mutes sound.

The story leads you through the valley: the meadows, the Gloomwood, the Frostfells, the Sunscar Desert, the Mirefen Bog and finally the barrow-downs. Each region is harder than the last. Monster levels are colored: red means dangerous, grey means too weak to give much XP.

Growing stronger: every level gives attribute points and a talent point. New abilities unlock at levels 3, 5 and 7. At level 10 you choose one of three specializations, with its own talent tree and a signature ability.

Cities and shops: walk into shops through their doors (E). Every shop buys your loot; each one sells its own kind of goods. Stables sell mounts (F to ride), General Stores sell potions and torches.

Dungeons and caves lie at fixed places in the world. Dungeon bosses drop gear made for your class and specialization. Caves are dark: bring a torch.

Professions (P): learn Blacksmithing, Leatherworking or Enchanting from city trainers and craft with materials from monsters. Skills: using a weapon trains its skill and makes its hits stronger; casting trains Spellcraft.

Home city: talk to the innkeeper in any city to make it your home. Your Hearthstone (H) teleports you there, and you wake there if you fall. Dying costs 10% of your level's experience (it can drop you a level) and a little weapon skill.

Lost? Read the waymarks along the roads (click them) and listen to the bards in each city.
