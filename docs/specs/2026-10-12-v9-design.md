# Emberfall v9: design spec

Date: 2026-10-12 · Status: approved in chat, awaiting spec review
Built in three deliveries, played and checked between each: **A** quick wins → **B** new look → **C** skills, keys, balance and co-op synergy.

Code lives in `src/*` and is built into `index.html` with `build.sh`. Test locally with `serve.ps1` (launch config "emberfall", port 8765). Co-op is tested with `?nettest=a` / `?nettest=b`.

---

## Delivery A: quick wins

### A1. Warning "!" instead of "?"
- `drawAwareness` (src/85-vision.js): while an enemy is noticing you (`alert > 0`, no target), it draws `!` instead of `?`. The colour goes from amber to red as `alert` rises, and the fill bar stays.
- On aggro (`aggroFlash`) it draws a larger red `!` with a short scale pulse (1.4× → 1× over 0.3 s).
- Signposts (`sign` decor) keep their `?`. Quest givers are unchanged.

### A2. Auto loot (personal setting, solo and co-op)
- Settings gets **Auto loot** (on/off, default off) and **Minimum item rarity** (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary; default Uncommon).
- These are stored in localStorage under `emberfall-settings`, shared by all heroes on that browser.
- Every 0.25 s, for each lootable corpse within `LOOT_REACH` of the player:
  - Gold, supplies and materials are always taken.
  - Items are taken only if `rar >= minRarity` and the bag has room. Otherwise they stay on the corpse.
- One summary toast per sweep, e.g. "+14 gold, Wolf Pelt, Green Ring". "Your bag is full" appears at most once every 10 s.
- **Co-op:** each player decides for themselves. A guest's auto loot sends the same `take` messages a click would, and the host stays authoritative. Loot stays shared, so the first to arrive takes it.
- No auto loot while dead, mounted-flying or inside a shop.

### A3. Area loot window
- Clicking a corpse or pressing E opens one window listing **every lootable corpse within `LOOT_REACH`**, with each corpse's loot under its own heading (the corpse's name).
- `lootOpen` becomes a list of corpses. **Take all** empties all of them. Clicking a row takes that one item.
- The window re-renders when the loot changes, drops corpses that are emptied or out of reach, and closes when none are left.
- Guests send `take` with each row's `cid`, as today.

### Testing A
Pull a mob and check the `!` stages. Kill a group of 3–4, then check area loot and Take all. Auto loot on and off with each rarity threshold, and with a full bag. Two-tab co-op: both players auto looting, one on and one off.

## Delivery B: new look ("minimal glass")

### B1. Visual system
- Dark translucent panels (`rgba(10,12,16,.62)` with a backdrop blur where supported), 8 px corners, a 1 px `rgba(255,255,255,.08)` hairline, and no gold rims or bevels.
- **Inter** (Google Fonts) for all UI text and numbers. Cinzel is kept only for big titles: the title screen, region banners and story screens.
- Readability: body text at least 14 px, labels at least 12 px, text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 against panels, and numbers in tabular figures.
- Colour roles: health `#e5484d`, mana `#4c8dff`, XP `#c9a227`, rarity colours unchanged, and level colours unchanged.
- All colours and sizes become CSS custom properties on `:root`, so the look is tuned in one place.

### B2. Player frame (top left), replacing the orbs
- A 56 px round **portrait**, then name and level, slim health and mana bars with "412 / 560" numbers, an XP line, and buff and debuff icons below.
- Low health pulses the frame edge red, replacing the orb pulse. The orbs and their CSS are removed.

### B3. Portrait
- `drawPortrait(canvas, look)` draws the hero's head and shoulders with the same drawing code as the in-game figure (skin, eyes, hair style, beard, headgear, or helm and hood from gear), scaled up and framed in a circle.
- It's cached per hero and redrawn when the look or gear changes.
- It's also used in party frames (friends' look is sent over the net once on join and on gear change) and on character select cards.

### B4. Target frame
- Same style, top centre: a monster portrait (reusing the corpse/creature drawing), its name, a level in its level colour, and health. Bosses get a wider bar.

### B5. Action bar and windows
- Action bar slots: thin outlines, the key label in a corner, a cooldown sweep with seconds, mana cost tint when unaffordable, and a glow when ready after cooldown.
- These windows are all restyled to the glass system with shared components (panel, header, tabs, button, row, tooltip): inventory, shop, talents, professions, map, quests, settings, friends, pause, loot and dialog.
- Floating combat text: crisper (Inter, outlined), crits larger.

### B6. UI size
- A **UI size** setting (80–130%, default 100%) scales the whole HUD through a CSS variable. It's stored in `emberfall-settings`.

### Testing B
Before and after screenshots of each window. Check each window at UI sizes 80% and 130% and at a 760 px width. Check the portrait with several looks (hood, helm, beard and bald). Check the party frame portraits in two-tab co-op.

- Numbers are starting points. The balance bench (C4) sets the final values.
- Each ability gets its own effect, built on the existing fx system (particles, rings, trails), and a synthesized sound.
- Level-up shows "New ability: X" and slots it into the first empty bar slot.

### C2. Spellbook (K) and action bar
- **8 slots** plus basic attack on the left mouse button. Default keys 1–8.
- The spellbook lists every class and spec ability with icon, description, live damage numbers, cooldown and mana. Locked ones are greyed with "Level N".
- Drag from the spellbook onto a slot. Drag between slots to swap, or drag off to clear. Swapping is blocked in combat (enemy targeting you): "Can't change abilities in combat".
- The loadout is stored per hero as `p.bar` (8 ability indexes or null). Old saves migrate as slots 1–4 → abilities 1–4, slot 5 → spec ability, and the rest empty.
- **Co-op:** guests send casts by ability index as today, so nothing changes in the protocol.

### C3. Keybindings
- Settings gets a **Keybindings** tab listing every action: move up/down/left/right, the 8 slots, potion heal/mana, food, mount, hearthstone, interact/loot, inventory, talents, spellbook, professions, map, friends, mute and pause.
- Click an action, press a key, and it's bound. A conflict prompts "Already used by X: swap?". There's also **Reset to default**.
- Stored in `emberfall-keys` (per browser). Escape can't be rebound.
- The keydown handler (src/50-game.js:249–264) changes from hard-coded `e.code` checks to an action lookup, and movement reads the bound keys.
- The action bar, hint line and tooltips show the bound keys.

### C4. Balance
- **Bench mode** (`index.html?bench`, developer only) runs each of the 12 specs automatically at levels 5, 10, 15 and 20, in standard gear for that level. It fights three fixed scenarios: a pack of 4 same-level mobs, one elite, and a boss.
- It records damage per second, time to clear, damage taken, potions used and deaths, and outputs a table.
- **Target:** every spec's clear time within ±10% of the median for each scenario and level, and no spec dying where others don't.
- Class identity is kept: Paladin tougher with lower burst, Sorcerer highest burst but fragile, Hunter safest at range, Necromancer strongest over time.
- Tuning is done through ability coefficients, cooldowns and class base stats, not item stats, so existing gear stays valid.
- Sorcerer gets particular attention as the known weakest class. The bench results are shown to Anders before and after.

### C5. Co-op synergy
- **Party auras** (radius 600 px, shown on the party frames):

  | Class | Aura |
  |---|---|
  | Paladin | Devotion: −8% damage taken |
  | Sorcerer | Brilliance: +15% mana regen |
  | Hunter | Keen Eye: +5% crit chance |
  | Necromancer | Dark Pact: 2% lifesteal |

  The same aura doesn't stack, so mixed parties get more auras. A solo hero gets their own class's aura (which is part of the balance baseline).
- **Cross-class combos** (also work solo with your own abilities):
  - Frozen: hits from any source get +30% crit chance.
  - Cursed: +15% fire damage taken.
  - Stunned: +25% damage taken.
  - Marked (Hunter's Mark): +25% damage taken from all sources (already part of the ability).
- A combo hit shows a short coloured label ("Shatter!", "Kindled!") so players notice it.
- The host is authoritative for status effects. Guests already receive enemy state, so the status flags (`frozenUntil`, `cursedUntil`, `stunUntil`, `markedUntil`) are added to the enemy sync.

### Testing C
The bench before and after tuning. A manual playthrough at level 12–20 for at least one spec per class. Spellbook drag and drop, combat lock and migration of an old save. Rebind every action and check conflicts and reset. Two-tab co-op: auras on the frames, and combos across players.

## Out of scope (parked)
Raising the level cap, new zones, the endgame (difficulty modes, raids), trade window and chat.
